# Mission Abundance · Episode 2 — The Clear Vision
### Protocol Step 2 · Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: focused, warm, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- This episode is about *clarity*: knowing exactly what you want and holding it. Speak with quiet certainty, the way you would describe a place you have already been.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[long pause]` = 4 seconds. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "The Clear Vision"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
**The Clear Vision.**
A deep dive into step two of the **Abundance Protocol.**

## 0:20 – 0:55 · From haze to focus
*A haze of scattered points slowly gathers into one sharp ring.*

A vague wish… stays a **wish.**
`[pause]`
You cannot move toward a **blur.**
Scattered wanting pulls you in a hundred directions… and takes you nowhere.
`[pause]`
But when the picture sharpens… everything begins to gather around it.

## 0:55 – 1:35 · Know exactly what you want
*A compass needle spins, searching, then settles on a star.*

Know **exactly** what you want.
`[pause]`
Not "more money." Not "a better life."
But **what** it buys… what it builds… who you become with it.
`[pause]`
Make it specific enough to **see.**
When you know your direction… the needle stops spinning.

## 1:35 – 2:10 · Write it down
*Lines of handwriting appear on a page that begins to glow.*

Write it **down.**
`[pause]`
Ink turns intention into something **real.**
One page. In the present tense. As if it is **already** done.
`[pause]`
Read it in the morning. Read it at night.

## 2:10 – 2:50 · Hold the completed picture
*Tiles of a picture appear one by one until the whole scene glows.*

Now… hold the **completed** picture.
`[pause]`
Not the steps. Not the obstacles. The **finished** scene.
`[pause]`
Where are you? Who is with you? What does the morning feel like?
See it. Feel it. **Dwell** there.

## 2:50 – 3:25 · Do not feed the obstacles
*A searchlight swings among gray shapes, then settles on the star; the shapes shrink.*

Do not dwell on **obstacles.**
`[pause]`
Attention feeds what it **touches.**
The lack, the doubt, the old story… they grow when you stare at them.
`[pause]`
Give your light to the **vision.**

## 3:25 – 4:05 · Hold it with gratitude
*The completed picture glows; warm light flows toward it from every side.*

Hold your vision with **gratitude.**
`[pause]`
Thank you… as if it is **already on its way.**
`[pause]`
Clarity plus gratitude… becomes **faith.**
And faith… moves you.

## 4:05 – 4:50 · Guided practice
*A breathing orb.*

Let's practice together. `[breath]`
Close your eyes. See **one scene** from your completed vision.
`[long pause]`
Add detail. Where you are. Who is with you. How it **feels.**
`[long pause]`
Breathe in… I see it **clearly.**
`[pause]`
Breathe out… **thank you.**

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
See it **clearly.**
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
