# Mission Abundance · Episode 5 — Unhurried
### Protocol Step 7 · Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: slow, spacious, grounded

**Delivery notes**
- This is the slowest episode. Let your pace *demonstrate* the message: longer pauses, softer endings.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "Unhurried"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
Episode Five. **Unhurried.**
Step seven of the **Abundance Protocol.**

## 0:20 – 0:55 · Hurry is fear in motion
*A clock whose hand spins fast, throwing sparks, then slows to a calm sweep.*

Hurry is **fear** in motion.
`[pause]`
It says: time is running out. There is not enough.
That is scarcity… wearing a **watch.**
`[pause]`
Notice the rush in your body. The tight chest. The quick breath.
`[pause]`
You do not have to **obey** it.

## 0:55 – 1:35 · Nature never hurries
*A slow tide beneath a drifting moon.*

Nature never **hurries**…
and yet, everything is **accomplished.**
`[pause]`
The tide arrives on time.
The seed takes the season it needs.
`[pause]`
You, too, are part of this rhythm.
There is a pace that is yours… and it is calmer than you think.

## 1:35 – 2:10 · Calm is clear
*A pond's ripples settle until a star appears in the still water.*

Before you act… **pause.** Breathe.
`[pause]`
Watch the water settle.
When the surface is still… you can see the stars in it.
`[pause]`
Calm is not slow. Calm is **clear.**
And a clear mind… sees **more.**

## 2:10 – 2:50 · Faith
*A lantern lights a few steps of a winding road; a faint star waits at the horizon.*

**Faith** is acting as one who is already **receiving.**
`[pause]`
You do not need to see the whole road.
A lantern lights only the next few steps.
`[pause]`
Take them.
And the road appears… as you **walk** it.

## 2:50 – 3:25 · Majestic assurance
*A slow sun rises behind mountain ridges.*

Move with calm, **majestic assurance.**
Like a natural law. Like the sun… rising on schedule.
`[pause]`
No strain. No drama.
**Steady** beats fast.

## 3:25 – 4:05 · Faith is not passive
*A sailboat raises its sail; wind fills it.*

Faith is **not passive.**
`[pause]`
The sailor raises the sail… and trusts the **wind.**
`[pause]`
Do your part completely.
Stay awake to what is **real.**
And let the rest… do its part.

## 4:05 – 4:50 · Breathing practice
*A breathing orb. Four slow breaths.*

Let's slow down together. `[breath]`
Breathe in… I am **unhurried.**
`[pause]`
Breathe out… everything is **moving.**
`[pause]`
Breathe in… I do my part.
Breathe out… I **trust** the process.

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
**Unhurried.**
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
