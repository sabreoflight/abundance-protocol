# Mission Abundance · Episode 4 — Give First
### Protocol Step 6 · Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: warm, open-handed, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- This episode is about *generosity as flow*, not sacrifice. Keep your voice light and kind. Never guilt the listener.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "Give First"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
Episode Four. **Give First.**
Step six of the **Abundance Protocol.**

## 0:20 – 0:55 · Abundance moves
*Two ponds: one only receives and grows still; one receives and gives, and stays bright.*

A pond that only takes… grows **still.**
A spring that gives… stays **fresh.**
`[pause]`
Abundance is not something you hold.
It is something that **moves** through you.
`[pause]`
And the movement begins with **giving.**

## 0:55 – 1:35 · Use-value
*A scale tips as a golden gift grows.*

Give more in **use-value**… than you take in **cash-value.**
`[pause]`
Use-value is what your gift is worth… **to the person who receives it.**
Not what it costs you. What it **does** for them.
`[pause]`
So ask: how can I be more **useful** today?

## 1:35 – 2:10 · Better off
*A traveling light leaves each point it touches glowing.*

Leave every person **better off.**
In **vitality.** In **usefulness.** In **opportunity.**
`[pause]`
Even a small kindness counts.
A clear answer. An honest encouragement. Your full attention.
`[pause]`
Walk through the day like a light that stays lit… in everyone it passes.

## 2:10 – 2:50 · Five ways to give
*Five lights ignite in a ring: Attention, Kindness, Skill, Time, Resources.*

You have more to give than money.
`[pause]`
**Attention.** Kindness. Skill. Time. And resources.
`[pause]`
Start with what is **already** in your hands.

## 2:50 – 3:25 · Give from overflow
*A cup fills with light, then overflows into two smaller cups.*

Give from **overflow**… not from depletion.
`[pause]`
Fill your own cup first… with **gratitude.**
When it is full, giving is simply the cup… running over.
`[pause]`
And healthy giving keeps its **boundaries.**
You are not asked to empty yourself. Only to share what overflows.

## 3:25 – 4:05 · Give freely
*A string between you and your gift dissolves; the gifts spread out, then light returns from every direction.*

Give **freely**… not as a trade.
`[pause]`
A gift with a string attached… is a transaction.
Release the **string.**
`[pause]`
Value that circulates… tends to **return.**
Often from places you did not expect.
`[pause]`
Your task is to give. Not to manage how it comes back.

## 4:05 – 4:50 · One gift today
*A breathing orb.*

Today… give **one gift.**
A message. A skill. An hour. A kind word.
`[pause]`
Give it fully, with no string.
`[pause]`
Now breathe with me. `[breath]`
Breathe in… I have **enough to give.**
`[pause]`
Breathe out… I give **freely.**

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
**Give first.**
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
