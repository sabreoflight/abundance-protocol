# Mission Abundance · Episode 6 — The Morning Practice
### Protocol Step 8 · Guided Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: intimate, slow, guided

**Delivery notes**
- This is a *guided practice*, not a lecture. Speak directly to one person, as if sitting beside them at dawn.
- Leave real silence after each instruction so the listener can actually do it. `[pause]` = 2 seconds. `[long pause]` = 4 seconds. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.
- On screen, a ring of seven lights represents the protocol. Each movement lights the next one.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "The Morning Practice"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
Episode Six. **The Morning Practice.**
Step eight of the **Abundance Protocol**… the step that holds all the others.

## 0:20 – 0:50 · How you begin
*Dawn: a sun rising slowly over a calm horizon.*

How you **begin**… shapes how you move.
`[pause]`
The first minutes of the day set the tone.
Before the world asks anything of you… **arrive.**
`[pause]`
This practice takes five minutes. Seven short movements… one for each step.
Sit, stand, or find a window. Let's begin.

---

## 0:50 – 4:06 · The seven movements
*The ring of seven lights lights up one at a time.*

**0:50 · One — Choose the lens**
**One.** Choose the **lens.**
`[pause]`
Say it quietly: today, there is **enough**… and more can be created.
`[long pause]`
Say it once more. Slowly.

**1:18 · Two — Hold the picture**
**Two.** Hold the **picture.**
`[pause]`
See what you are building today. Clearly. **Completed.**
`[long pause]`
Hold it for three slow breaths.

**1:46 · Three — Gratitude**
**Three.** **Gratitude.**
Name three things you already have.
`[long pause]`
Say thank you for each.
`[pause]`
Now add one more… in **advance.**
Thank you… for what is on its way.

**2:14 · Four — Release the contest**
**Four.** Release the **contest.**
Think of one person you might compare yourself to.
`[pause]`
**Bless** them. Sincerely.
Their light is welcome. There is room for us all.

**2:42 · Five — The daily work**
**Five.** The daily **work.**
Choose three things for today. Just three.
`[pause]`
Today is complete… when these are done.
One at a time. **Fully.**

**3:10 · Six — Give first**
**Six.** **Give first.**
Choose one gift for today. A message. A skill. A kind word.
`[pause]`
Give it **freely**… with no string.

**3:38 · Seven — Unhurried**
**Seven.** **Unhurried.**
Slow your breath. Slow your pace.
`[pause]`
Do your part completely… and **trust** the process.

---

## 4:06 – 4:50 · The affirmation
*All seven lights glow around a breathing orb. Each line appears on its own.*

Now… say these words with me. Slowly. `[breath]`
`[pause]`
I choose **abundance.**
`[pause]`
My vision is clear. My gratitude is **constant.**
`[pause]`
I release the contest. I give **first.** I move **unhurried.**
`[pause]`
There is **enough.** There is **more to create.**
I **begin.**

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
**Begin** today.
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
