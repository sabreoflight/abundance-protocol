# The Masterclass Manual — Voice-Over Script
**Runtime:** 7:24 · **Pace:** ~100 words per minute · **Tone:** warm, low, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- Speak as if sitting beside one person at dawn. Never push.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = a slow, audible in-breath. Let the animation carry the pauses.
- **Bold** words are the ones that appear and glow on screen. Lean on them gently, without raising your volume.
- Drop your pitch slightly at the end of each sentence. Leave the last line of each scene a half-beat longer than feels natural.
