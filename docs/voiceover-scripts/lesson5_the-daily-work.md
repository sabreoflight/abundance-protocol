# Mission Abundance · Episode 3 — The Daily Work
### Protocol Step 5 · Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: steady, warm, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- This episode is about *quality of attention*, not quantity of effort. Keep your voice grounded and kind. No hustle language.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "The Daily Work"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
Episode Three. **The Daily Work.**
Step five of the **Abundance Protocol.**

## 0:20 – 0:55 · Thought becomes action
*A staircase of light; a small light climbs toward a distant star.*

Thought is the **creative power.**
`[pause]`
But thought alone stays a **picture.**
Action walks it into the world.
`[pause]`
Not frantic action. Not endless toil.
Simply the **next step**… taken with the vision in view.
`[pause]`
One step. Then the next.

## 0:55 – 1:35 · Every day is complete
*Thirty dots light up, one for each day.*

Every day is **complete in itself.**
`[pause]`
You are not judged by one heroic day… but by the **accumulation** of days.
So finish today's work… **today.**
Leave nothing half-done for tomorrow to carry.
`[pause]`
Thirty days. Thirty small successes.
And a life begins to change shape.

## 1:35 – 2:10 · One thing at a time
*Scattered orbits settle; one light moves to the center.*

Do **one thing** at a time.
Give it your complete attention.
`[pause]`
Hurry splits attention. Calm **gathers** it.
The task in front of you is the only task there is.
`[pause]`
This is the Certain Way: not more effort… but more **presence.**

## 2:10 – 2:45 · Put growth into every act
*A point of light sends out slow rings.*

Put **growth** into every act.
Writing. Building. Replying. Serving.
`[pause]`
Whatever you are doing, do it with the conviction that it is moving you **forward.**
`[pause]`
Nothing is small… when it is done with **purpose.**

## 2:45 – 3:25 · Be a source of increase
*Light travels outward through a network of people.*

Be a source of **increase.**
Leave every interaction better than you found it.
`[pause]`
Your work is your **signature.**
Service well given… has a way of **returning.**
`[pause]`
Let each person you touch feel that they have **gained.**

## 3:25 – 4:05 · Do the work, release the grip
*A seed becomes a plant, rising into light.*

Do the work **fully.**
Then loosen your grip on **how**… and **when.**
`[pause]`
A gardener plants, waters, and clears the weeds.
The gardener does not pull the shoot to make it grow faster.
`[pause]`
Seeds grow while you rest.
Trust the soil… and keep **tending.**

## 4:05 – 4:50 · A daily rhythm
*A sun crosses the sky; three small stones wait on the ground.*

A simple rhythm for each day. `[breath]`
In the **morning**… choose three things. Just three.
`[pause]`
At **midday**… one thing at a time. Fully.
`[pause]`
In the **evening**… close the day. Three things completed… and one thank-you.
`[pause]`
Then rest. Tomorrow is another complete day.

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
Do today's work… **completely.**
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
