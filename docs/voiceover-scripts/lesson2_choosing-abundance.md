# Video 2 — Choosing Abundance
### Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 · Pace ~95 wpm · Tone: steady, warm, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- Speak as a calm presence beside the listener, not a coach. Never hype.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = an audible, slow breath. In the practice, match your breathing to the orb (4 seconds in, 4 seconds out).
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen. Lean on them gently.
- The heart of this video is **gratitude** (2:30–4:15). Let your voice soften there.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings and rising light. Title: "Choosing Abundance"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
This is Episode One of Mission Abundance.
**Choosing Abundance**… in every scenario.
Especially the **hardest** ones.

## 0:20 – 1:00 · Two lenses
*Left: a pie that shrinks with every slice. Right: a spring that never runs dry.*

There are two ways to see the same room.
`[pause]`
Through **scarcity**, there is only so much. A pie. And every slice you take… leaves less for me.
Money, opportunity, recognition — even happiness — all begin to feel like a contest.
`[pause]`
Through **abundance**, you see a spring. Value is created… not divided. There is always more to make… and you can make it.
`[pause]`
Same room. Same facts. A different lens.
And the lens is a **choice.**

## 1:00 – 1:25 · The power of focus
*Scattered rays warm nothing. A lens gathers them into one flame.*

Attention is **light.**
Scattered, it warms nothing.
`[pause]`
Gathered into one point… it **ignites.**
Whatever you focus on… grows. Brighter. Larger. More real in your life.

## 1:25 – 1:50 · Today is one frame
*A film strip. The first frame is dim; each frame after it brightens as focus moves along.*

Even when today shows lack — a thin balance… a slow season…
notice: today is one **frame.** Not the whole film.
`[pause]`
It is real… and it is **not final.**
Keep your focus on the **next frame** you are building.

## 1:50 – 2:30 · In hard seasons
*A window slowly fills with warm light.*

Choosing abundance is easy when life is easy. This practice is for the hard seasons.
`[pause]`
It is **not denial.** Hard seasons are real.
You can acknowledge what is difficult… and still open the window.
`[pause]`
Abundance does not deny what is missing. It decides where your attention rests **next.**
`[pause]`
**Notice.** `[pause]` **Name** it honestly. `[pause]` Then… **give thanks.**
Gratitude is where your attention rests next.

---

## 2:30 – 4:15 · Gratitude — the way from lack to abundance
*Warm green-gold palette. Eyebrow on screen: "GRATITUDE"*

**2:30 · The bridge** *(a gray island of "lack"; stepping stones of light form a bridge to a glowing island of "abundance")*
Feeling abundant… does not wait for **more.**
`[pause]`
It begins with **gratitude.**
Gratitude is the **bridge**… from lack to abundance.
`[pause]`
Even before the facts have changed.

**3:00 · Name three things** *(three lights ignite one by one; the whole field brightens)*
Name **three things** you already have.
Small counts. A breath. A kind word. A skill. A roof.
`[pause]`
Each one is a light.
Together… they **change the room.**

**3:30 · Give thanks in advance** *(a warm sun radiates; light flows inward)*
Now give thanks **in advance.**
Be grateful for the abundance already on its way to you.
`[pause]`
Gratitude will not let you accept lack… as **permanent.**

**3:55 · Gratitude overflows** *(a spiral of new light grows from one point)*
Gratitude **overflows.**
Into giving. Into creating. Into more.
`[pause]`
Every small creation is proof: there is **more.**

---

## 4:15 – 4:50 · Breathing practice
*A breathing orb. Four slow breaths.*

Let's practice together. `[breath]`
Breathe in… I already have **enough.**
`[pause]`
Breathe out… **thank you.**
`[pause]`
Breathe in… there is **more on its way.**
Breathe out… I choose **abundance.**

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
*A single steady light. Fade.*

Choose **abundance.**
Again… and again.
Begin with **gratitude.**
`[breath]`
