# Mission Abundance · Episode 2 — Release the Contest
### Protocol Step 4 · Voice-Over Script · Runtime 5:00 (+7s intro, +10s credits) · Tone: steady, warm, unhurried

**Delivery notes**
- Calm presence, no hype. This is about *freedom*, not self-criticism. Never shame the listener for comparing; almost everyone does.
- `[pause]` = 2 seconds of silence. `[breath]` = a slow audible breath. In the practice, 4 seconds in, 4 seconds out.
- **Bold** words are the ones glowing on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Opening
*Breathing rings. Title: "Release the Contest"*

Take a slow breath. `[breath]` `[pause]`
Welcome to **Mission Abundance.**
Episode Two. **Release the Contest.**
Step four of the **Abundance Protocol.**

## 0:20 – 1:00 · The invisible race
*Lanes of light; orbs endlessly overtaking one another on a treadmill.*

Most of us are running a race we never entered.
Comparison turns life into a **race.**
`[pause]`
And a race has **one winner.**
`[pause]`
**Scarcity** whispers: *"If they rise… I fall."*
`[pause]`
But notice: this race has **no finish line.**
Nobody ever arrives.

## 1:00 – 1:30 · The quiet cost
*Your light is pulled sideways toward other lanes, then returns home.*

Attention spent on **their lane**…
is light taken from **your own.**
`[pause]`
Every glance sideways is a moment not spent on what you are **building.**
`[pause]`
So call your attention home.

## 1:30 – 2:05 · Step off the track
*The lanes fade. The light rises into an open field under the stars.*

Step **off the track.**
`[pause]`
There is an **open field** here.
No lanes. No finish line. Room for **everyone.**
`[pause]`
This is the **Creative Plane.**
You do not win here… you **make.**

## 2:05 – 2:45 · Their light is welcome
*One candle lights another, then many.*

When someone else succeeds, it is not taken from you.
Their success is **proof**… not a threat.
It shows what is **possible.**
`[pause]`
Joy in another's good… is **abundance** in practice.
One flame can light another… and lose **nothing.**

## 2:45 – 3:25 · Desire by creating
*One light becomes two, then four, then many.*

Desire the best for yourself.
But desire it by **creating** it.
`[pause]`
Make more. Don't take more.
`[pause]`
One idea becomes two. Two become **many.**
Creation has no **ceiling.**

## 3:25 – 4:10 · The practice: notice, name, bless
*A gray light of comparison softens and warms when blessed.*

When the contest creeps in, three small steps.
`[pause]`
**Notice** the comparison.
Think of one person you compare yourself to.
`[pause]`
**Name** it, gently. No judgment.
`[pause]`
Then **bless** them. Sincerely.
Silently wish them well… and watch your own light grow.

## 4:10 – 4:50 · Breathing practice
*A breathing orb. Four slow breaths.*

Let's breathe together. `[breath]`
Breathe in… there is **room** for all of us.
`[pause]`
Breathe out… I release the race.
`[pause]`
Breathe in… their light is **welcome.**
Breathe out… I create **my own.**

## 4:50 – 5:00 · Closing
Release the **contest.**
Choose **abundance.**
`[breath]`
